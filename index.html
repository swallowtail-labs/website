Today

Chips are flat.

Separate dies, wired side by side, millimetres apart. Moving data can cost 1000x more energy than computing on it.

3D

Stack them.

Bond the dies directly and the wiring shrinks from millimetres to micrometres. Faster, smaller, more power efficient.

Bottleneck

The heat is trapped.

Inner layers have no surface to shed heat.

Our approach

Inverse solving.

Design tools never put the physics in the loop. We train a model of the physics, then work backwards from the target to the design.
